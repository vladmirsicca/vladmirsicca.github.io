Objectifs :  The first part of this course focuses on the real functions of several real variables and particularly on optimization without constraints and under constraints of equality and inequality. Existence theorems, necessary conditions and sufficient conditions of optimality are studied. The second part of the course is devoted to the study of differential systems: linear and non-linear systems, notion of equilibrium and stability of equilibria.
